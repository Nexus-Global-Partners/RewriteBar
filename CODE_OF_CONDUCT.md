# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Focus criticism on the work, assume good intent, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub.
